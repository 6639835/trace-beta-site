What to test

Please focus on capture accuracy, HTTPS decryption stability, and Compose behavior on iOS and macOS.

  • Start and stop capture repeatedly. On iOS, test Tunnel mode after approving the VPN configuration; on macOS, test transparent capture after approving the NetworkExtension configuration. Check that normal connectivity returns after stopping.
  • Install and trust the Trace CA using Certificate & Trust, enable HTTPS decryption, and inspect HTTP/1.1, HTTP/2, HTTPS, WebSocket, and Server-Sent Events traffic. Report missing requests, incorrect headers or bodies, dropped connections, and decryption failures.
  • Check compressed response bodies, including gzip, deflate, Brotli, and Zstandard, against the expected content.
  • In Compose, create, send, and replay requests. Test collections, environments, variables, authentication, pre-request scripts, post-response scripts, and tests. Compare the sent request and displayed response with your expected result.
  • Import HAR, cURL, Postman, Insomnia, and OpenAPI data. Test HAR and cURL exports and collection exports in Trace, Postman, OpenAPI, and Markdown formats.
  • Exercise host, map, rewrite, script, block, and breakpoint rules with traffic you are authorized to inspect.
  • If you have both platforms, pair your iPhone or iPad with a Mac and test Mac Display capture sync. Test Internet Exit separately, including its permission on the Mac, then switch back to This Device.
  • Report crashes, hangs, unexpected battery or network behavior, and localization problems through TestFlight. Include the platform, steps to reproduce, expected result, and actual result. Review captures before sharing because they can contain sensitive data.
